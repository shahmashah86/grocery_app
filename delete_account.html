Account Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Open the app and go to the "Person" section.
  2. Select "Delete Account".
  3. Confirm your decision by accepting the terms displayed.

Data That Will Be Permanently Deleted:

  • Personal Information: (e.g., name, email, password, etc.)
  • Usage History
  • Stored Preferences

Data That Will Be Retained (For Legal Compliance):

  • Transaction Records: Retained for 6 months
  • Support Correspondence: Retained for 1 year

Note: Retained data is securely stored and is not linked to your identity after account deletion.
