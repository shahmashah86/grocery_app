Account Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your account and associated data, please follow the steps below:

  1. Open the app and navigate to the "Person" section.
  2. Select "Delete Account".
  3. Confirm your decision by agreeing to the terms presented.

Upon confirmation, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Personal information (e.g., name, email, password etc..)
  • Usage history
  • Any stored preferences

Data that will be retained for legal purposes includes:

  • Transaction records (retained for 6 months)
  • Support correspondence (retained for 1 year)
