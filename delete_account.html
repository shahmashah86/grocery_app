Account Deletion Instructions

Users can delete their account directly from the Fresh Mart app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Fresh Mart app.
  2. Go to the "Person" section in the app settings.
  3. Tap on "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm your decision by accepting the terms displayed.

Important Information:

Once your account is deleted:

  • Personal Information (e.g., name, email, password) will be permanently erased.
  • Usage History and Stored Preferences will be deleted.
  • Any **remaining transaction records** will be retained only for legal compliance purposes.
